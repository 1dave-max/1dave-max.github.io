AinaraClipper

AinaraClipper is a personal automation tool built on the open-source Ainara AI assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps prepare short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) from footage provided by content campaigns the owner has joined.

What it does

  • Cuts and captions short clips from campaign footage on the owner's computer.
  • After the owner approves a clip, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedules it, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads the owner's channel name to confirm it is posting to the right channel.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
